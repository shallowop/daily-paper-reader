## 摘要
若干乳制品零售商共享一个冷藏冷链网络，各自将产品分配到不同热敏感性分组中，以最大化二次-分式效率（DEA 风格）。该公式度量单位物流成本对应的腐败风险，并将技术效率与规模效率分离。单智能体问题是一个非凸分式 QCQP，而多零售商博弈形成一个带有共享风险预算约束的广义纳什均衡问题（GNEP），通常是 NP 难的。在 Arrhenius 腐败定律支配下，热风险协方差继承了一种秩一加对角结构，从而释放出隐藏凸性。作者通过联合 Cholesky-谱变换提出 HSDI（Hidden SOCP Dinkelbach Iteration）算法。主要贡献包括：1. 建立无需矩阵可交换性的同时对角化。2. 推导精确二阶锥规划（SOCP）松弛的显式充分条件，并给出非精确情形下的闭式次优性界。3. 证明全局收敛性：外层 Dinkelbach 循环在精确情形下 Q-超线性收敛，在非精确情形下 R-线性收敛；耦合变分均衡（VE）迭代在强单调性下 R-线性收敛，达到总体复杂度，同时确保原始-对偶对的 Lipschitz 稳定性。在越南和美国乳制品市场校准后，HSDI 无需随机重启即可匹配多起点非线性基线的目标值，并在精确性条件下产生全局最优解。HSDI 的经验缩放指数为 0.93（SLSQP 为 1.30），提供了一个经认证的多项式时间框架，以及用于优先安排冷链投资的稳定影子价格。

## Abstract
Several dairy retailers share a refrigerated cold-chain network, each allocating products across heat-sensitivity subgroups to maximize quadratic-fractional efficiency (DEA style). This formulation measures spoilage risk per unit of logistics cost, separating technical from scale efficiency. The single-agent problem is a non-convex fractional QCQP, while the multi-retailer game forms a generalized Nash equilibrium problem (GNEP) with a shared risk-budget constraint, which is generally NP-hard.   Governed by the Arrhenius spoilage law, the thermal-risk covariance inherits a rank-one-plus-diagonal structure that unlocks hidden convexity. The authors propose the HSDI (Hidden SOCP Dinkelbach Iteration) algorithm via a joint Cholesky-spectral transform.   Key contributions include:   1. Establishing simultaneous diagonalization of without requiring matrix commutativity.   2. Deriving an explicit sufficient condition for exact second-order cone programming (SOCP) relaxation, alongside a closed-form suboptimality bound for inexact regimes.   3. Proving global convergence: the outer Dinkelbach loop converges Q-superlinearly (exact) or R-linearly (inexact), and the coupled variational-equilibrium (VE) iteration converges R-linearly under strong monotonicity, achieving an overall complexity while ensuring Lipschitz stability of the primal-dual pair.   Calibrated on Vietnam and US dairy markets, HSDI matches the objective value of multi-start non-linear baselines without random restarts, yielding global optima under exactness. HSDI exhibits an empirical scaling exponent of 0.93 (versus 1.30 for SLSQP), offering a certified polynomial-time framework and a stable shadow price for prioritizing cold-chain investments.
