## 摘要
凯斯勒综合征一词最初用于描述失控的轨道级联过程，该过程会形成高密度碎片场。本文将该思想一般化并应用于供应链。加性渠道使故障能够催生更多故障；减性渠道则侵蚀系统遏制和恢复的能力。当损害速度超过修复速度时，故障变得不可逆，系统会进入一种新状态。供应品将理论与应用连接起来。库存使各子系统彼此隔离，而库存耗尽则会暴露依赖关系。对波斯湾国家而言，2026年春季起对海上供应的限制无法由航空和公路运输替代方案充分补偿。这会在不同产品间不均衡地消耗库存，并损害物流系统本身的运行。吞吐量优化在各处积累脆弱性；海湾地区正是其变得可见之处，而霍尔木兹海峡的限制是触发因素。在最弱环节收益下将其表述为凯利投资组合配置时，最优策略将资源集中于落后而非领先的投入要素。

## Abstract
The term Kessler syndrome was coined for a runaway orbital cascade creating a high-density debris field. The idea is generalised and applied to supply chains. An additive channel enables failures to generate further failures; a subtractive channel erodes the capacity to contain and restore. When damage outpaces repair, failure becomes irreversible and the system settles into a new state. Supplies connect the theory to its application. Stocks insulate subsystems from one another, and their exhaustion exposes dependencies. For the Persian Gulf states, restrictions on maritime supplies from spring 2026 are inadequately compensated by air and road transport alternatives. This depletes inventories unevenly across products and impairs the functioning of the logistics system itself. Throughput optimisation builds fragility everywhere; the Gulf is where it is becoming visible, with the Strait of Hormuz restriction as the trigger. Formulated as a Kelly portfolio allocation under a weakest-link payoff, the optimal strategy concentrates resources on the lagging rather than the leading inputs.
