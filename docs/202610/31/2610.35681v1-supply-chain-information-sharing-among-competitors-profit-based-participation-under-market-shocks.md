## 摘要
信息共享有助于协调供应链决策以应对市场冲击。然而，企业必须在这些收益与增强竞争对手实力的风险之间进行权衡。我们研究竞争企业何时会自愿加入信息共享平台，以交换关于市场冲击的信息，同时保留运营自主权。企业在信息能力上存在差异，并在私人信号实现之前决定是否参与，从而形成随机信息不对称。我们将参与决策和产量决策建模为不完全信息下的两阶段动态博弈。对于两个横向竞争者，我们推导出闭式均衡参与策略，揭示信息实力和生产效率如何影响参与决策。数值分析考察更复杂的供应链网络。我们发现供应链特征会影响参与决策，而信息不对称程度是均衡平台构成中的关键因素。信息实力相近的企业可能受益于联合起来，以减轻相对于信息实力更强竞争对手的竞争劣势。成本效率较低的企业可能不太愿意参与，因为其竞争对手可以利用共享信息；而不确定性可以通过限制更高效竞争对手的行动而充当竞争保护盾。然而，当多个竞争对手加入平台时，成本效率较低的企业也可能选择参与，以避免处于信息劣势。

## Abstract
Sharing information can help coordinate supply chain decisions in response to market shocks. Yet, firms must weigh these benefits against the risk of strengthening competitors. We study when competing firms voluntarily join an information-sharing platform to exchange information about market shocks while retaining operational autonomy. Firms differ in information capabilities and decide whether to participate before private signals are realized, creating stochastic information asymmetry. We model participation and quantity decisions as a two-stage dynamic game with incomplete information. For two horizontal competitors, we derive closed-form equilibrium participation strategies that reveal how information power and production efficiency shape participation. Numerical analysis examines more complex supply chain networks. We find that supply chain characteristics impact participation and the level of information asymmetry is a key factor in the equilibrium platform profiles. Firms with similar information power may benefit from joining forces to reduce their competitive disadvantage against higher information power competitors. Cost-inefficient firms may be less willing to participate because their competitors can exploit shared information, and uncertainty can serve as a competitive shield by restricting the actions of more efficient competitors. However, when several competitors join the platform, a cost-inefficient firm may also choose to participate to avoid an informational disadvantage.
