## 摘要
本研究构建了一项国家层面的衡量指标，用于评估欧洲北约盟国在战略压力下动员、补充、维持和持续保障军事能力的经济-工业能力。该指标包含四个组成部分：宏观经济能力、部门韧性、国防工业能力以及后勤与保障。分析覆盖28个欧洲盟国，并在平衡基线与五种地缘经济压力情景下进行评估。结果将基线能力与压力脆弱性区分开来，并提供了一个评估框架，用于识别可能制约持续军事准备的经济-工业瓶颈。基线得分在国家之间差异显著，样本均值为0.610，范围为0.471至0.759。经校准的地缘经济冲击在每种情景下都会降低有效能力，但影响高度异质。综合压力情景的平均影响最大，使样本均值降至0.491，较基线下降19.5%。TRS和PR主要通过国防工业与补充约束发挥作用；供应链碎片化主要由部门暴露和可替代性驱动；外部支持减少则通过盟国准入与持续保障发挥作用。我们的结果表明，应将注意力从单纯的支出目标转向将资源转化为可用军事能力的能力：关键问题不仅在于支出多少，还在于哪些瓶颈限制了资源向能力的转化。

## Abstract
This study develops a country-level measure of the economic-industrial capacity of European NATO Allies to mobilise, replenish, maintain, and sustain military capability under strategic stress. The measure comprises four components: macroeconomic capacity, sectoral resilience, defence-industrial capability, and logistics and sustainment. The analysis covers 28 European Allies and evaluates a balanced baseline against five geoeconomic stress scenarios. The results distinguish baseline capacity from stress vulnerability and provide an assessment framework for identifying the economic-industrial bottlenecks that can constrain sustained military readiness. Baseline scores vary materially across countries, with a sample mean of 0.610 and a range from 0.471 to 0.759. The calibrated geoeconomic shocks reduce effective capacity in every scenario, though the effects are highly heterogeneous. The combined-stress scenario has the largest average effect, lowering the sample mean to 0.491, a decline of 19.5 per cent relative to baseline. TRS and PR operate mainly through defence-industrial and replenishment constraints; supply-chain fragmentation is driven primarily by sectoral exposure and substitutability; and external-support reduction operates through allied access and sustainment. Our results suggest shifting attention from expenditure targets alone towards the capacity to convert resources into usable military capability: the key issue is not only how much is spent, but also which bottlenecks limit the conversion of resources into capability.
