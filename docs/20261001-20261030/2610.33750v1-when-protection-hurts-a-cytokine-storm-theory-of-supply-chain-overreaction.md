## 摘要
供应链韧性研究传统上将安全库存、备用供应商、加急运输和紧急采购等防御性行动视为有益。然而，COVID-19大流行、2021年半导体短缺和苏伊士运河堵塞等中断事件表明，同步囤货、重复下单和产能预订会放大拥堵和运营成本，有时甚至加剧原始中断。受此观察启发，我们构建了一个决策理论框架，其中保护性响应强度（Protective Response Intensity, PRI）是内生决策变量。我们引入响应诱发损失（Response-Induced Loss, RIL）来刻画防御性行动产生的成本，并刻画了一个在缓解中断与自诱发损失之间取得平衡的唯一最优响应。我们推导出一个闭式关键响应阈值，超过该阈值时RIL超过原始中断强度，这一现象称为*供应链细胞因子风暴*。我们进一步提出细胞因子风暴指数（Cytokine Storm Index, CSI），衡量响应诱发损失相对于原始中断的大小，并讨论利用运营数据对其进行潜在校准。将框架扩展到相互关联的供应网络，我们推导出一个谱稳定性条件，用以区分有界的分散响应与网络范围传播。数值实验表明，最优响应保持在风暴阈值以下，而比例响应启发式可能超过该阈值。网络响应根据推导的稳定性条件收敛或发散。总体而言，我们的发现强调，供应链韧性不仅取决于缓解中断，还取决于使防御性响应与其所要应对的冲击保持成比例。

## Abstract
Supply chain resilience research has traditionally viewed defensive actions such as safety stock, backup suppliers, expedited transportation, and emergency sourcing as beneficial. However, disruptions such as the COVID-19 pandemic, the 2021 semiconductor shortage, and the Suez Canal blockage highlight how synchronized hoarding, duplicate ordering, and capacity reservation can amplify congestion and operational costs, sometimes worsening the original disruption. Motivated by this observation, we develop a decision-theoretic framework in which Protective Response Intensity (PRI) is an endogenous decision variable. We introduce Response-Induced Loss (RIL) to capture costs generated by defensive actions and characterize a unique optimal response that balances disruption mitigation against self-induced losses. We derive a closed-form critical response threshold beyond which RIL exceeds the original disruption intensity, a phenomenon termed a *Supply Chain Cytokine Storm*. We further propose the Cytokine Storm Index (CSI), measuring response-induced loss relative to the original disruption, and discuss its potential calibration using operational data. Extending the framework to interconnected supply networks, we derive a spectral stability condition distinguishing bounded decentralized responses from network-wide propagation. Numerical experiments show that optimal responses remain below the storm threshold, whereas proportional response heuristics can exceed it. Network responses converge or diverge according to the derived stability condition. Overall, our findings highlight that supply chain resilience depends not only on mitigating disruptions but also on keeping defensive responses proportionate to the shocks they are intended to address.
